Renderer and React component: `createCanopy` (`./three`) draws the grove with an orthographic turntable, DOM titles and topic names on their reserved discs, picking without raycasts, selection and search dimming, an outline tree for assistive technology, and render on demand. `<Voronoi3D>` (`./react`) wraps it with controlled or uncontrolled props.
